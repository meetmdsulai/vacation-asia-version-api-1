# Domain Boundaries
Primary capabilities: Customer/Agency, Sales/CRM, Quotation, Hotel, Transport, Ferry, Sightseeing, Meal, Guide, Package, MICE, Pricing, Booking, Traveller/Rooming, Operations, Payment/Finance, Supplier, Notification, Documents, Administration/Audit.

Cross-domain engines: Itinerary, Availability, Coverage, Personalization, Cancellation and Rules. They orchestrate through contracts and do not take ownership of domain data.
Operations is a work/execution layer across confirmed bookings; it does not own product master data.
