# Payment Schedule, Credit and Receivables Rules
- PAY-SCH-001 Partial payment is mandatory: an approved quotation becomes a booking only after at least 50% of travel cost is successfully paid/verified.
- PAY-B2C-001 B2C can pay the initial amount by supported online payment or bank transfer. Remaining balance can be paid through supported online payment, bank transfer or cash-in-hand operations.
- PAY-B2B-001 Agency/Distributor also pays at least 50% upfront. Remaining balance may be due after trip completion under approved credit arrangements.
- CRD-001 Credit is available only to credibility-proven/approved agencies/distributors, not universally.
- CRD-002 Credit facility may be percentage-based or fixed monetary amount and must be configurable.
- AR-001 Finance must track outstanding receivables separately from successful payment transactions.
- PAY-MAN-001 Bank transfer and cash require controlled manual verification/recording; they are not gateway-success events.
- PAY-FUT-001 `TakeIndiaMarket` is an intentionally unresolved future topic. Never infer or implement requirements from the code word until Product Owner supplies them.
