# Payment & Finance Rules
Approved quotation requires initial payment >=50% to become booking.
B2C: initial >=50%; remaining may be online, bank transfer or cash under supported operations/current policy.
Agency/Distributor: initial >=50%; remaining may be collected after trip under eligible credit arrangements.
Credit is credibility-based, not universal, and may be percentage or fixed monetary amount.
Bank transfer/cash need manual verification/recording in addition to online gateway.
Finance User can process refund; retain amount, reason, original payment, affected component, charges/refundable amount, actor/time, provider reference/status.
TTB is initial provider but remains behind provider abstraction.
