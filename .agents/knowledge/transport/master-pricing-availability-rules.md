# Transport — Master, Pricing & Availability Rules

- TRN-MST-001 — Maintain vehicle-type capability separately from actual vendor vehicles.
- TRN-MST-002 — Maintain transport vendors, drivers and physical vehicles for later operational allocation.
- TRN-MST-003 — Geographic zones/areas support routing, eligibility and restrictions; do not depend on hotel-name keyword matching.
- TRN-MST-004 — Airports are associated with service cities/areas for automatic itinerary-leg generation.
- TRN-MST-005 — Ferry routes/schedules are first-class transport configuration.
- TRN-MST-006 — Routes can represent land or multimodal movements and participate in feasibility.
- TRN-RULE-001 — Pax-to-vehicle rules produce eligible/recommended combinations, then luggage/route restrictions can narrow/override them.
- TRN-PRC-001 — Transport pricing is driven by configured service/mode/route/vehicle rules; do not invent pricing from vehicle master data.
- TRN-PRC-002 — Daily-basis overtime is a configured extra-hour charge after the standard service duration.
- TRN-AVL-001 — Actual vehicle/driver/vendor availability is an operational concern and can be assigned later than quotation/booking requirement selection.
