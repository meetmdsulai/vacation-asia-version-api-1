# Transport Rules
Modes include Airport Pickup/Drop, Standby, Daily/Day-to-Day, Sightseeing-only and Ferry where applicable.
Current vehicle examples: 2-3 Car; 4-9 Van; 9-11 combinations; 12-14 two Vans; larger groups may require Coach. Treat these as configurable rules, not scattered constants.
Eligibility considers pax, luggage, route/area restrictions and preference. Customer selects eligible preference; Operations later assigns vendor/vehicle/registration/driver.
Airport pickup generates arrival Airport->Hotel and departure Hotel->Airport. Multi-city flight travel can generate airport/hotel segments; permitted routes may be removed.
Standby can cover whole trip/selected days. Daily standard duration is 10h; extra hours use configurable pricing. The 10h rule does not apply to Standby.
Normal transport price includes standard operating costs described by business; avoid double charging.
Operational confirmation uses a global configurable lead-time before service.
Route restrictions override pax recommendation. Durable implementation must not rely on hotel-name keyword scanning.
Ferry is sellable, pre-scheduled and supports SIC/Private; schedule also affects route feasibility.
