# Operational Substitution Rules
- OPS-SUB-001 If an operational supplier cannot fulfil after booking, Operations may source and assign a substitute without involving the customer.
- OPS-SUB-002 Substitute must preserve the confirmed customer-facing service specification/equivalence. Material downgrade or materially different purchased product is an amendment/escalation, not a silent substitution.
- OPS-SUP-001 Phase 1 supplier communication may be email/phone with staff recording confirmation; future Supplier Portal updates the same underlying workflow rather than creating a parallel process.
