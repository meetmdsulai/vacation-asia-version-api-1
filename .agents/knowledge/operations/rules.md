# Operations Rules
Post-booking operations: traveller/rooming readiness, Transport confirmation, vendor/vehicle/driver allocation, Guide allocation, restaurant reservation, arrivals/departures, vouchers and exceptions.
Hotel/Sightseeing confirm before quotation approval; Transport/Meal can confirm after booking.
Operations may substitute an unavailable supplier without customer involvement only when delivered service remains equivalent to confirmed specification. Material downgrade/change requires amendment/escalation.
Queues should surface due/overdue work based on service dates and configured thresholds.
