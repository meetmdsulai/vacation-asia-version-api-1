# Cross-Domain Availability & Coverage Resolution

- AVL-001 — Availability is domain-owned. Package/Personalization queries domain contracts; it does not manipulate another domain's tables.
- AVL-002 — A product shown as available can still require supplier confirmation according to business phase/rules.
- AVL-003 — Hotel and Sightseeing supplier confirmation are mandatory gates for quotation approval.
- AVL-004 — Transport and Meal confirmation are deferred to post-booking operations.
- AVL-005 — Coverage must be evaluated before adding duplicate services.
- AVL-006 — Sightseeing-included pickup/drop prevents duplicate Transport charging/allocation for the covered route.
- AVL-007 — Standby transport can cover sightseeing transport requirements; Daily Basis presents a choice to use the daily vehicle where applicable.
- AVL-008 — Complimentary hotel/sightseeing meals satisfy Meal Coverage for the relevant day/meal type.
- AVL-009 — Route feasibility includes timing, vehicle restrictions and multimodal/ferry transitions.
- AVL-010 — Final availability used for approval/booking must be snapshot/auditable enough to explain the decision later.
