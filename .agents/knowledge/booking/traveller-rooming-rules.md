# Traveller, Group and Rooming Rules
- TRV-001 Passenger types/age boundaries are supplier/product specific; rates resolve against supplier definitions.
- TRV-002 Quotation can begin with counts/ages needed for pricing; full traveller details are collected after the initial payment creates the booking.
- TRV-003 One booking may contain multiple hotels, multiple stays in one city and multiple room types in one hotel.
- TRV-004 Group rooming lists are supported and allocated after payment/booking.
- TRV-005 Guide is optional for 20 pax or fewer and may be requested even for small FIT; Guide is mandatory above 20 pax regardless of FIT/Group/MICE label.
