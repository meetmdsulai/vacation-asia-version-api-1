# Booking & Amendment Rules
- Approved quotation becomes booking only after >=50% initial payment is successful/verified.
- Support multiple hotels, repeated city stays and multiple room types.
- Full traveller details and rooming list are post-payment.
- Customer cannot directly edit confirmed booking; raise change request and authorized Admin applies permitted amendment.
- Hotel change after booking is restricted/non-refundable under current policy. Sightseeing/Meal/Transport may change within thresholds/rules.
- Amendment reprices as a financial delta and preserves original history.
