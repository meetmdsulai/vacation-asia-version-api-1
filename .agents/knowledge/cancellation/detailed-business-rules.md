# Cancellation Detailed Business Rules
- CAN-001 Cancellation eligibility is threshold/time/policy driven and can differ by supplier/component.
- CAN-002 Calculate Hotel, Sightseeing, Transport and Meal cancellation independently using the applicable supplier refund/cancellation configuration.
- CAN-003 Show the customer a component-level cancellation/refund breakdown before cancellation is committed.
- CAN-004 Aggregate component results into total cancellation charge and total refundable balance.
- CAN-005 Hotel is currently non-refundable for the described post-booking hotel-change scenario; general cancellation must still resolve against the applicable approved supplier/business policy.
- CAN-006 Finance User is authorized to process refunds; refund actions remain auditable and linked to original payment/component/cancellation calculation.
