# MICE Rules
MICE is a first-class substantial event/large-group domain, not just a flag. Match accommodation plus meeting/event needs.
Hotels with ballrooms are matched by ballroom capacity; external venues/ballrooms independent of hotels are supported.
Future needs can include layouts, AV/equipment, catering/gala dinner, multiple coaches/guides and event schedules. Exact detailed rules remain NEEDS_CLARIFICATION until approved.
