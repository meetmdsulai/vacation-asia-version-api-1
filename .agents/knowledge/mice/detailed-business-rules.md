# MICE Detailed Business Rules
- MICE-001 MICE is a substantial large-group/event domain, not merely a booking label.
- MICE-002 MICE matching considers accommodation plus conference/meeting/ballroom capacity requirements.
- MICE-003 A suitable venue may be a hotel with an adequate ballroom or an external event/ballroom venue independent of accommodation.
- MICE-004 Guide rule remains pax-based: above 20 pax Guide is mandatory. MICE size can be very large; multiple-guide allocation formula remains NEEDS_CLARIFICATION.
- MICE-005 Detailed conference layouts, AV/equipment, catering/gala dinner and event operations are planned capabilities but are not approved detailed rules until separately specified.
