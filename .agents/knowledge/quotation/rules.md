# Quotation Rules
- New quotation enters review.
- Hotel and Sightseeing must confirm before approval.
- Website inventory does not eliminate Hotel reconfirmation: room quantity may exceed allocation or supplier may decline; this safety step may be automated later.
- Transport and Meals do not block approval.
- If Hotel rejects, Admin cannot silently replace it; suggest alternative, obtain customer agreement, revise quotation, then reconfirm.
- Default expiry is 3 days and configurable.
- Revisions retain history; never overwrite previous commercial versions.
