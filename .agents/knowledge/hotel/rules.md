# Hotel Rules
Hotel is a mandatory pre-booking supplier-confirmation component. Search may show allocated rooms, but Admin reconfirms acceptance before quotation approval.
Support multiple hotels, repeated city stays, multiple room types and post-payment group rooming.
Passenger/child pricing is supplier-specific; no universal age rule.
Hotel complimentary meals participate in Meal Coverage. Hotel cancellation/change may be non-refundable according to applicable supplier/business policy.
