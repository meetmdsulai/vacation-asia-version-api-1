# Meal Rules
Meal is coverage-driven: purchase only when Hotel/Sightseeing/package does not already provide it. Included coverage may still require menu selection at zero customer charge.
Meal selection is for the entire group; do not create per-traveller menu selections. One group does not choose different restaurants for the same meal service.
Restaurant confirmation is operationally required but may occur after booking.
Menu behavior can include ITEM_ACCUMULATE, CATEGORY_RULE and FIXED. Dietary/allergen data may warn/filter while selection remains group-level.
