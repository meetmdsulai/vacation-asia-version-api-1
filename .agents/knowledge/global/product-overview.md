# Product Overview
Vacation Asia is an itinerary-driven travel quotation, booking, operations and distribution platform for B2C, agencies and distributors. It supports Hotel, Transport/Ferry, Sightseeing, Meals, Guides, Packages and MICE, with supplier confirmation, pricing, payment, amendments, cancellation/refunds and fulfilment operations.
It is not purely instant-booking: supplier confirmation and operational fulfilment are first-class concerns.
