# Screen Architecture Principles
Admin navigation should be work-oriented: Dashboard, Sales, Bookings, Operations, Products, Commercial, Finance, CRM, Administration.
Booking Workspace is the central internal screen aggregating Overview, Itinerary, Travellers/Rooming, Hotel, Transport, Sightseeing, Meals, Guide, Pricing, Payments, Documents, Communications and Activity without transferring domain ownership.
Operations Board surfaces arrivals/departures, pending confirmations, allocations, reservations and exceptions.
Finance requires Dashboard, Payments/Attempts, Schedules, Bank/Cash Verification, Receivables, Agency Credit, Refunds, Reconciliation/Settlements, Invoices/Receipts and Audit.
