# Current Screen Architecture
Status: DRAFT SCREEN ARCHITECTURE — business capabilities are approved where backed by rule IDs; exact screens/navigation are not yet finalized.

Candidate workspaces:
- Customer/B2C: search/package/personalization, quotation status, approved quotation/payment, booking, traveller details, rooming inputs where appropriate, trip/itinerary, change request, cancellation simulation, payment history.
- Agency/Distributor: quotation/booking plus account balance, eligible credit and outstanding invoices/receivables.
- Admin/Sales: quotation review/approval checklist, revisions, pricing override with audit, customer communication.
- Booking Workspace: overview, itinerary, travellers/rooming, Hotel, Transport, Sightseeing, Meals, Guide, Pricing, Payments, Documents, communications/activity.
- Operations: supplier confirmations, transport confirmation queue, vehicle/driver allocation, Guide allocation, restaurant reservation, rooming readiness, arrivals/departures, exceptions.
- Finance: dashboard, payments, payment attempts, schedules, bank/cash verification, receivables, agency credit, refunds, reconciliation, settlements, invoices/receipts, audit.
- Product: Hotel, Transport/Ferry, Sightseeing, Meal, Guide, Fixed/Personalized/Custom Package, MICE venue/event configuration.
- Supplier Portal: requests, confirmations, bookings/upcoming services, amendments/cancellations, vouchers and applicable finance/documents.
- Administration: users/roles, master/configuration, notification/document templates, audit.

Do not treat this file as final UI specification. Final Screen Registry must define Screen ID, role, purpose, data/actions, business rules, entry/exit and owning domains.
