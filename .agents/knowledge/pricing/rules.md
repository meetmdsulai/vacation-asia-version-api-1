# Pricing Rules
FCT is primary commercial/pricing owner. Supplier rate is internal. Markup examples discussed: Agency 14%, B2C 20%, Distributor 12%; all configurable, never constants.
VAT may be inclusive/exclusive. External users see only final authorized price.
Sales/Admin can override price but must preserve calculation, adjustment, reason, actor/time and final value.
Personalized/Custom always reprice from components.
